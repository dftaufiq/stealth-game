Lanternkeep is a top-down stealth game: sneak past the night watch, steal the signal lenses, and reach the exit unseen — across three levels at a lighthouse, with guard vision cones blocked by cover, a sweeping lighthouse beam, security cameras, hearing, pebble distractions, bushes to hide in, and a detection meter that gives you a chance to recover.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak, Space to run, click or press Q to throw a pebble, E to pick up, R to retry a level, P to pause, M to mute.
